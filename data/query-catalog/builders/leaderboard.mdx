---
title: "Builder Leaderboard"
description: "Rank builders by V2-attributed share-notional volume, fills, or fees."
---

Canonical reference: [Builder Leaderboard](/data/builders/list)

```bash
curl "https://api.polynode.dev/v3/builders?sort=volume&limit=1" \
  -H "x-api-key: $POLYNODE_API_KEY"
```

`sort=volume` uses locally indexed outcome-share par notional, matching Polymarket's builder-volume convention for CLOB V2 history. Supported sorts are `fills`, `volume`, and `fees`.
