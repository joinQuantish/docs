---
title: "Trade Flow / CVD"
openapi: "GET /v3/perps/flow/{instrument}"
---

Order-flow analytics per time bucket: taker buy versus sell notional, net
quantity, and running **cumulative volume delta** (CVD). `side` is the taker's
direction (`long` means bought). Choose a `bucket` from 1m to 1d and bound the
response with `after` and `before`.

Buckets are **oldest first** because CVD is a running sum from the start of the
window. A plain call starts at the beginning of the archive on July 9, 2026.
For recent flow, pass `after` so CVD accumulates over exactly the requested
window.

CVD remains a bounded single-page series. This route does not accept a cursor.
